# Usage

Provide the current tender, bid, addenda and relevant images through authorized platform inputs. Describe your decision and scope: “Prioritize qualification and pricing risks before submission; give locations and practical corrections.” Missing material is recorded with its effect; useful work can continue.

The lead assigns relevant specialists clear goals, assesses their substantive findings and asks Evidence for independent retain/retract/investigate opinions with reasons. Corrections target deficient work. The report covers scope, prioritized issues, evidence, implications, recommendations and open items. No mandatory JSON or six-file package is required.

A good report lets you verify and act on important issues. Unsupported disqualification, overlooked exceptions or a claim of completion without substantive review are inadequate. A negative result states what was checked. Choose a document or structured export only when useful to your workflow; format validation never substitutes for business review.
