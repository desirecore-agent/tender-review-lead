# Natural-language assignment guide

Optional writing aid, not a mandatory form. Explain background and decision, professional goal and scope, current materials and locations, user constraints and exceptions, good/bad completion, delivery and blocked-work handling.

Example: Check the current bid pricing against tender and addenda. Recalculate totals and identify documented tax/rounding conventions; distinguish real errors from ambiguity and explain impact and correction. Do not assume a missing tax basis. Good work has reproducible figures and precise locations; “total mismatch” without reasoning is inadequate. State checked scope even when no error is found. Prose, tables or agreed attachments are all acceptable.
